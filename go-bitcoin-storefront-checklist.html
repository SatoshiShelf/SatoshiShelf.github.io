FAQ

Is this checklist really free?
Yes. There is no email gate, no account, and no download form. Print the page or save it as a PDF from your browser.
Do I need every item to launch?
No. The five-item “if you only do five” list covers the minimum. The rest reduces the odds of a support ticket, a leaked file, or a payment you cannot reconcile.
Does this apply to Lightning?
Most of it does. Wallet handling, pricing, delivery, and policy still matter. Lightning adds channel liquidity and routing, so test that path separately before claiming support.
What should I do after launch?
Take one real payment, watch the full flow, and ask the buyer what was confusing. Fix that before adding more products or another payment rail.
