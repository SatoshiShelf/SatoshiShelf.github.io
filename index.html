FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I pay?
Use any Lightning-enabled Bitcoin wallet. The checkout creates a Lightning invoice and shows a QR code; your wallet fills in the exact amount. If you prefer on-chain, there is a fallback address and txid verification.
What if I don't have Bitcoin?
You can buy Bitcoin in minutes from any reputable exchange in your country, then send it from your own wallet. There are no country restrictions here.
How fast do I get my product?
As soon as the Lightning payment settles, usually in seconds, the download link appears on the page. The on-chain fallback unlocks once the transaction is seen — no waiting for email.
Do I need an account?
No. There's no signup, no email required, and nothing stored. Buy, pay, download.
Can I try before I buy?
Yes. Every product has a one-time 12-second free preview — open it, look around, then decide. Each preview can only be used once per device.
Can I get a refund?
These are digital files delivered instantly, so all sales are final. If a file is broken or missing, message us on Telegram and we'll make it right.
Is my payment private?
Lightning payments are not published on the Bitcoin blockchain. The store's LNbits backend can see invoice metadata, but we don't collect personal data or card details. On-chain fallback payments are public.
