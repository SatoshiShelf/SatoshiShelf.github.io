₿ On-chain checkout ⚡ Instant delivery 🔒 No account needed
Updated 28 October 20263 minute readBy Satoshi Shelf

Satoshi Shelf is a small, independent store for practical digital downloads. Pay in Bitcoin on-chain, get the file, and keep it. No account, no card, and no email required.

What this is

The shelf holds 179 products across 20 categories: spreadsheets, templates, contracts, Python scripts, browser tools, planners, and games. They are built for people who want a useful file without a subscription, a marketplace account, or a checkout that asks for more information than the sale needs.

Every product has a free 12-second preview so you can see what you are buying before you pay. Product files are private; the public storefront does not expose the downloadable archives.

Why Bitcoin

Bitcoin makes a small direct sale possible without a card processor in the middle. That matters for three reasons:

  • Access. A buyer with a wallet can pay from anywhere, even when a card is unavailable or a processor does not serve their country.
  • No account. There is no signup, no password, and no customer profile to manage.
  • Direct settlement. On-chain payments go straight to the store's Bitcoin address. The checkout does not hold a customer balance or require a top-up.

It also has trade-offs. Bitcoin payments are irreversible and the buyer pays the network fee. On-chain payments are public on the blockchain and slower than a card or Lightning payment; the checkout accepts a mempool sighting so delivery does not wait for a block. The guides document the payment flow, the failure modes, and the decisions behind the store rather than pretending those trade-offs do not exist.

How delivery works

  1. Open a product page and choose Pay with Bitcoin.
  2. The checkout shows a Bitcoin address and QR code with the exact BTC amount, converted from the USD price at the live rate.
  3. Pay from any Bitcoin wallet, then paste the transaction ID into the checkout.
  4. The store checks the transaction server-side against the store address and unlocks a short-lived download link.
  5. The file is yours. There is no expiry on the product itself and no subscription.

If a payment is verified but the download does not unlock, message @xsaifsaifx on Telegram with the product name and the on-chain transaction ID.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or an email address to buy.
  • We do not ask for card details.
  • We do not install analytics, advertising pixels, or account-tracking scripts.
  • We do not promise anonymity. On-chain payments are public: the store can see the amount, address, and timing, but does not tie them to an identity. The store avoids collecting unnecessary customer data.

The policies page explains delivery, refunds, privacy, and the limits of the product licenses in plain language.

Who runs it

Satoshi Shelf is run by Saif. It is an independent project, not a marketplace with a support department. The storefront is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages, and the product files and payment verification run separately from the public pages. That keeps the store light, fast, and free of an account database.

The best way to reach a human is Telegram. Product questions, broken files, and custom-work requests all go to the same place.
