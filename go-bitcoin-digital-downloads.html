Questions

Bitcoin buying, answered plainly

Which wallets work?

Any Bitcoin wallet that can scan a QR code or send to an address — on-chain. Receive-only wallets won't work, since you're sending.

Do I need to hold a whole Bitcoin?

No. Products start at $1.99 and the checkout shows the exact amount in BTC at the live rate. You pay the network fee separately, as always.

Is the price fixed in USD?

Yes. The USD price is fixed and converted at checkout, so you always know what you're paying.

What if my transaction is still unconfirmed?

You get access as soon as the payment is seen in the mempool; confirmation just follows on its own.

Is my payment private?

There's no email, card or personal data collected. You pay a public address — nothing ties the purchase to your identity on this side.
