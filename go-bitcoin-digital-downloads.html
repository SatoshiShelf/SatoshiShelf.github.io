Questions

Bitcoin buying, answered plainly

Which wallets work?

Any Lightning-enabled Bitcoin wallet that can scan a QR code or open a BOLT11 invoice. If you only have an on-chain wallet, use the fallback address at checkout.

Do I need to hold a whole Bitcoin?

No. Products start at $1.99 and the invoice is denominated in sats at the live rate. You pay any routing fee separately; on-chain buyers pay the network fee.

Is the price fixed in USD?

Yes. The USD price is fixed and converted when the invoice is created. The invoice is time-limited, so pay the amount shown.

What if my payment is still pending?

Lightning payments usually settle in seconds. The checkout keeps polling and unlocks when it does. If the invoice expires, create a new one.

Is my payment private?

There's no email, card or personal data collected. Lightning invoices are not published on-chain, though the store's LNbits backend can see invoice metadata. The on-chain fallback is public.
