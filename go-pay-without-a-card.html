When you have no card

The situations this actually solves

Cards declined or unsupported

Some countries, banks and prepaid cards get refused by card processors regardless of your balance. There is no card step here to fail.

Under 18, or no bank account

No age gate tied to a card and no bank requirement. Nothing asks for an ID document.

You don't want to hand over card data

Subscriptions you forgot, processors that leak, "just save your card for next time" — none of it applies when there's no card field.

Privacy-minded buyers

No account, no email, no tracking pixels, no purchase history tied to you on this end.

The mechanics

What replaces the card

Instead of a card number, you send Bitcoin to a self-custody address. The store verifies the transaction on-chain and unlocks your file.

QR or address

Scan with any wallet app, or copy the address into the wallet you already have.

Exact amount shown

The page converts the USD price at the live rate, so you send the right amount the first time.

Paste your txid

Your wallet gives you a transaction ID. Paste it back and the download unlocks automatically.

Don't have Bitcoin yet?

The short honest version

You'll need a small amount of BTC first. Any major exchange works — buy a few dollars, then withdraw to a wallet app on your phone.

How little can I buy?

Products start at $1.99. Buy the equivalent plus a little for network fees and you're set for several purchases.

Do I need to verify my identity anywhere?

Not on this store — there is no identity step. What an exchange asks is between you and them.

Which products are worth starting with?

The $1.99 arcade games are the cheapest way to test the whole flow end to end. If that works for you, everything else works the same way.
