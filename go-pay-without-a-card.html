When you have no card

The situations this actually solves

Cards declined or unsupported

Some countries, banks and prepaid cards get refused by card processors regardless of your balance. There is no card step here to fail.

Under 18, or no bank account

No age gate tied to a card and no bank requirement. Nothing asks for an ID document.

You don't want to hand over card data

Subscriptions you forgot, processors that leak, "just save your card for next time" — none of it applies when there's no card field.

Privacy-minded buyers

No account, no email, no tracking pixels, no purchase history tied to you on this end.

The mechanics

What replaces the card

Instead of a card number, you send Bitcoin to the store's address. The store checks the transaction server-side and unlocks your file.

Bitcoin QR

Scan the address with any Bitcoin wallet. No Lightning channel, no special wallet app, no account.

Exact amount shown

The page converts the USD price at checkout, so you pay the right amount the first time.

Paste the transaction ID

After sending, paste the txid into the checkout. The server verifies it and unlocks the download.
