Plain-language summary, not legal advice. These policies describe how Satoshi Shelf operates. Nothing here limits a right you have under the consumer law that applies to you.

Delivery

Products are digital files. There is no shipping and no physical item. After checkout, the store verifies the Bitcoin transaction on-chain and unlocks a short-lived download link. The link is scoped to the product you bought and is intended to be used promptly.

If the payment confirms and the download does not unlock, contact @xsaifsaifx on Telegram with the product name and transaction ID. Do not send a screenshot of your wallet seed phrase, and never share a private key.

No email address is required for delivery. If you close the page before downloading, contact support with the transaction ID and we will help you recover access.

Refunds

Because the products are digital files delivered immediately, all sales are final. This is the same rule shown on the product pages and at checkout.

If a file is broken, missing, or not the file described on its product page, contact support and we will fix the delivery or make it right. If you paid twice for the same product, or paid the wrong amount, contact support with the transaction details. Those cases are handled individually.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or limit any non-waivable consumer right that applies to your purchase.

Privacy

Satoshi Shelf does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or card details to complete a purchase. The store does not install analytics, advertising pixels, or account-tracking scripts.

Bitcoin payments are public by design. When you pay, the transaction, addresses, and amount are visible on the blockchain. The store may retain the transaction ID, product, amount, and timestamp for delivery and reconciliation. It does not need a customer profile to deliver a file.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard request data under GitHub’s privacy statement. The pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, which makes a request to Google under Google’s privacy policy. Links to YouTube, Instagram, GitHub, and Telegram take you to third-party services with their own policies.

If you contact support through Telegram, that conversation is handled by Telegram. Please do not send sensitive personal information, wallet seed phrases, or private keys.

Terms

License. Unless a product page says otherwise, a purchase grants you a license to use the files for personal or internal business purposes. You may not resell, redistribute, publish, or sublicense the files as a standalone product, template pack, or download.

No outcome guarantee. The products are practical tools, not a promise of income, employment, legal compliance, or any particular result. Business, financial, legal, and tax decisions remain yours. The content is not financial, legal, or tax advice.

Pricing and payment. Prices are shown in US dollars. The Bitcoin amount is quoted at checkout and is time-limited. The buyer pays the Bitcoin network fee. A payment that does not match the quoted amount or the stated network may not unlock a download.

Availability and changes. Products, prices, and these policies may change. The version published on this page applies to a purchase made after it is posted. If a product is removed, existing buyers can still contact support for access help.

Acceptable use. Do not use the store to violate sanctions, fraud, consumer-protection, tax, or other applicable law. The store may refuse or reverse a transaction where required by law.

Contact

For delivery help, refund questions, or press requests, message @xsaifsaifx on Telegram. Include the product name and transaction ID for order questions. Support is best-effort, usually within a day or two.
